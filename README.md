# Halfmoon Games Studio

> **Art · Heritage · Technology** — Where art comes to life.

Halfmoon Games Studio is a Dominican-Mexican studio that builds immersive digital experiences — augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and interaction — for museums, galleries, and cultural spaces, with a parallel line of original video game development.

🌐 [halfmoongamestudio.com](https://halfmoongamestudio.com/)

## English

### Who we are

We are a Dominican-Mexican studio that creates immersive digital experiences — augmented reality, virtual reality, and interaction — for museums, galleries, and cultural spaces, alongside our own video game development.

### What we do

Three capabilities, one mission.

| Area | Focus | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Spectra** | Augmented Reality & VR | AR/VR experiences that bring collections, artworks, and spaces to life. |
| **Cultura** | Museums & galleries | Interactive museography for institutions, heritage, and exhibitions. |
| **Games** | Original video games | Game development with its own narrative, art, and identity. |

### Featured project — LMH Art Collection

**Project / LMH Art Collection (2026)**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Interactive QRs | 14 |
| AR scenes | 5 |
| Time per experience | 2 min 30 sec |
| Year | 2026 |

- **Challenge:** Bring living artworks into the exhibition space and let visitors view sculptures that could not be moved because they were on display in other museums.
- **Solution:** Three living paintings were created that came to life when scanned through Halfmoon Spectra, plus two AR sculptures that visitors could view as if they were present in the center.
- **Result:** An excellent reception from visitors and a differentiating experience.

> "Four works. Four pioneers of modernity in Dominican art. Digital experiences that make *Visual Genealogy: Thresholds of Modernity* a more educational and playful exhibition, designed for the enjoyment of every kind of audience."
> — **Maria Elena Ditrén**, Exhibition Curator

### Proyecto destacado — LMH Art Collection

**Proyectos / LMH Art Collection (2026)**

| Métrica | Valor |
|---------|-------|
| QRs interactivos | 14 |
| Escenas AR | 5 |
| Tiempo por experiencia | 2 min 30 seg |
| Año | 2026 |

- **Reto:** Traer al espacio de la exposición obras vivas y que se pudieran observar esculturas que no podían moverse, ya que se encontraban expuestas en otros museos.
- **Solución:** Se realizaron 3 pinturas vivas que, al escanearlas a través de Halfmoon Spectra, cobraban vida, y dos esculturas en AR con las que los visitantes podían visualizarlas como si estuvieran dentro del centro.
- **Resultado:** Una excelente acogida de los visitantes y una experiencia diferenciadora.

> "Cuatro obras. Cuatro precursores de la modernidad en el arte dominicano. Experiencias digitales que hacen de *Genealogía visual: umbrales de la modernidad* una muestra más didáctica y lúdica, pensada para el disfrute de todo tipo de público."
> — **Maria Elena Ditrén**, Curadora de la exposición
